Costume Add-Ons: Grow Pleat, Grow Room, Hairpiece & Bra Cups

How customers choose them, how they're charged, and what you'll see.

The big picture: Customers can now add four optional extras to a custom order — Grow Pleat ($30), Grow Room ($10), Hairpiece ($30), and Bra Cups ($21.50), each per dancer. The whole point of this update: every add-on gets charged exactly once, before the customer pays — so we never silently miss a charge or accidentally bill twice.

Where a customer can choose an add-on

There are three moments they can turn an add-on on. They all feed into the same single charge — pick it in any one place and it's handled.

1

When they place the order (intake form)

The customer can tick the add-ons right on the order form. This is their earliest chance.
2

On the measurement form

Grow Pleat / Grow Room can also be chosen here, per the team's measurements (this is how it always worked — still works).
3

On the sketch approval page — the last chance

When the customer reviews and approves their sketch, the add-ons show up as on/off switches. Whatever they picked earlier shows up already turned on. This is the final moment to add or remove before they pay.

How charging works (and why it can't double up)

Behind the scenes, each grow add-on is one line item per costume order. No matter how many of the three places a customer touches it, the system keeps it to a single charge — it recognizes an add-on that's already there and won't add a second one.

Charging always happens before payment (at sketch approval, or when the measurement form is submitted while the invoice is still open).

Once an invoice is paid, add-ons are frozen. If a customer tries to add or change grow after paying, the system blocks it and emails support@ instead of silently changing a paid invoice. You'd then handle that case manually.

Who can see Grow Pleat & Grow Room

Grow add-ons only make sense for certain orders, so they only appear when the order qualifies:

Add-onShows when…
Grow Pleat ($30)Sport is Twirling, Color Guard, or Cheerleading and the costume has a short or long skirt
Grow Room ($10)Sport is Twirling, Color Guard, or Cheerleading (any costume)
Hairpiece ($30)Any custom order
Bra Cups ($21.50)Any custom order

Excluded: Impact Dance orders never show any add-ons.

What you'll see on the sketch approval page

This is the page where you (and the customer) finalize the order. For the grow add-ons:

  • Each eligible add-on shows as an on/off switch with its price.
  • If the customer already picked it (at intake or on the measurement form), the switch is already ON and a note reminds them it's their last chance to remove it.
  • They can turn one on to add it, or off to remove it — until they approve.
  • The total updates live so everyone sees the real price before approving.
If the invoice was already created (for example, orders that invoice early), the grow switches appear locked with a note to contact us — because changing them wouldn't reach an invoice that's already out.

The bottom line for you

You don't have to remember to charge for add-ons anymore. The customer's choice carries all the way through to the sketch page, shows up there for a final confirm, and gets billed once — automatically, before payment. Your main job is just to glance at the sketch page to confirm the add-ons look right before approving, and to handle the rare "they want it after paying" email if it comes up.
